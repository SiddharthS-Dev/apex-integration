# APEX Enterprise Sales Funnel, Journey and Buying Decision

_Inspironics APEX · Commercial Strategy · November 2026. Internal sales enablement._

> Readiness: capabilities marked LIVE are built and tested; none is yet proven in a customer production environment. No customer results, ROI, certifications or prices are claimed.

Stages 1–5 are marketing-led, 6–12 sales-led, and 13–15 customer-success-led. **The paid pilot (stage 9) is the pivot:** earlier stages earn the right to a pilot, and later stages turn pilot evidence into a contract.

### Pilot design (recommended)
- 8–12 weeks, paid, on the customer's own infrastructure (self-hosted).
- One or two modules, one or two teams, a named champion (persona C) and a sponsor (persona A or B).
- Written success criteria agreed before the start, measured from APEX's own analytics and completion records, against a baseline taken before the pilot.
- Weekly check-ins, then a pilot report the customer signs off, with a go/no-go on the licence.

## Engagement strategy

| Segment | Entry persona | Lead with | Opening message | First offer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering & tech services | Head of Engineering / L&D | Academia | “Your playbooks, taught and certified.” | Playbook-to-course pilot |
| Solution & system integrators | Head of Pre-sales / Marketing | Showcase + SlidesVault | “Show your portfolio; win with the right deck.” | Portfolio & deck library pilot |
| B2B tech sales teams | VP Sales | SlidesVault | “Every deck, findable and measured.” | Sales library pilot |
| Self-hosting / IP-sensitive | CIO / CTO | Front Door & Core | “Your data stays yours.” | Technical review + on-prem pilot |
| Partners & integrators | Partner principal | All modules | “Add a platform and services revenue.” | Partner enablement |

- **Top-down:** Executive briefing names a sponsor; sponsor opens doors to B, C and D.
- **Middle-out:** A department head with acute pain runs a pilot; results go up to A.
- **Technical-first:** CIO-led review for self-hosting buyers; security brief before demo.
- **Partner-led:** Integrator introduces APEX inside an existing client relationship.

## Persona demonstrations

| Persona | Length | Show | Proof point | Avoid | Close on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Executives | 15 min | Dashboard, one playbook becoming a verified certificate, analytics | Public certificate verification link | Settings and admin depth | Agree a pilot sponsor |
| B Senior Managers | 30 min | Team usage, compliance view, recertification after a playbook change | Compliance CSV export | Architecture detail | Nominate pilot teams |
| C Ops Leaders | 45 min | Playbook import → AI draft → quality flags → approval → quiz; meeting → action items | Coverage map back to source chapters | Pricing | Champion the pilot |
| D Technology | 45 min | Self-hosted install, identity and roles, audit log, proxied storage, AI budgets | Security controls walkthrough | Unbuilt features as if live | Technical sign-off |
| E End Users | 10 min | One sign-in, search, deck viewer, offline, a short lesson | Find a deck in seconds | Admin screens | Join the pilot team |
| F Partners | 30 min | Install, demo modes, content conversion workflow | Repeatable implementation steps | Customer names | Partner agreement next steps |

**Demo rules:**
- Use demo modes or a clean environment; label sample data as illustrative.
- Show only LIVE features as working; present roadmap items as roadmap.
- Open with the persona’s problem; end with one clear next step.
- Never quote customer results or ROI until a pilot report exists.
