# APEX Customer Persona Intelligence

_Inspironics APEX · Commercial Strategy · November 2026. Internal sales enablement._

> Readiness: capabilities marked LIVE are built and tested; none is yet proven in a customer production environment. No customer results, ROI, certifications or prices are claimed.


The six personas below refine the initial categories against what APEX actually does. APEX is bought by organisations whose value is expert know-how, so the buying group centres on executives, functional heads (engineering, sales and pre-sales, L&D), technology leaders and the people who run and use content daily.

## Buying roles

| Buying role | Who plays it |
|---|---|
| Economic Buyer | CEO / MD (Persona A) |
| Business Decision Maker | VP / BU Head (Persona B) |
| Technical Evaluator | CIO / CTO / IT (Persona D) |
| Business Influencer · Champion | L&D / Pre-sales / Ops lead (Persona C) |
| End User | Engineers and consultants (Persona E) |
| Procurement / Governance | Procurement, InfoSec, Legal, Finance |

**Procurement / governance stakeholder** (Procurement, Information security, Legal / Data protection officer, Finance): concerns are Data protection and residency (GDPR, DPDP); Contract, liability and support terms; Security questionnaire; Total cost of ownership. They need: Security & governance brief; Data processing terms; Commercial proposal with clear scope. *Be honest:* Personal-data export and erasure are on the roadmap; disclose and plan them in the contract.
