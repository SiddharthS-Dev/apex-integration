Unique selling points

What makes Apex different.

1

Three products, one front door

A showcase, a deck library and an academy behind one sign-in and one sign-out, at one address, with live status for each. Point tools keep these apart.

2

Playbooks become certified skills

Playbook to course to test to certificate, with re-certification when the playbook changes.

3

Tests learners cannot game

Graded on the server; answers never reach the browser; certificates are publicly verifiable.

4

Your files stay in your Dropbox

Every byte is proxied by Apex's servers; Dropbox tokens are encrypted at rest.

5

AI drafts, people decide

AI is optional, budget-capped and quality-checked; nothing reaches learners unapproved.

6

Light and self-hosted

A zero-dependency gateway, one command to start, closed to the network by default.

7

Tested before it ships

567 automated tests, server-side access checks, and zero open dependency advisories.
