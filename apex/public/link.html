If you already have an account in the Innovation Showcase or SlidesVault, you can link it to your Apex account, so Apex opens it for you. To prove both accounts are yours, you type your Apex password, then that app's own email address and password. Nothing is matched by email or name, and both passwords are only checked, never stored.

Linking changes nothing in the app account itself, and gives you no new permissions.
